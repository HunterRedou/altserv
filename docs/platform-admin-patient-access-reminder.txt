REMINDER: platform_admin patient data access
=============================================

Decision for now (agreed during Step 6):
- A platform_admin must NOT get automatic access to patient health data.
- Patient access is allowed only for:
  * chef: all patients in the chef's own tenant
  * employee: only patients with a currently valid, non-revoked assignment
  * platform_admin: only one named patient under an active, tenant-approved
    break-glass grant
- platform_admin may manage tenants and users, but not patient data by default.

Why:
- doc 4: "Plattform-Admin erhaelt nicht automatisch Zugriff auf Gesundheitsdaten."

Break-glass access (Step 3):
- Implemented in migration 023_break_glass.sql and internal/breakglass.
- A platform_admin requests one named patient in one named tenant with a reason.
- A chef of that tenant approves for 1 to 60 minutes, or denies.
- The requester or the tenant chef can revoke at any time.
- The only data route is read-only: GET /api/break-glass/grants/{id}/patient.
- The grant row belongs to the patient's tenant. RLS applies.
- Every request, decision, revocation, list and read is audited in that tenant.
- The free-text reason stays in the grant row. It is not copied to audit
  metadata.
- There is no tenant-wide grant and no hidden superuser path.

Review this decision later:
- Check the support verification process: who confirms the ticket before a chef
  approves?
- Check the grant review process: are open grants monitored and closed?
- Check the retention rule for break-glass evidence after a patient or tenant is
  deleted.
- Do not weaken the default rule for all platform admins.
- Check this file when platform-admin features, support tooling, or cross-tenant
  reporting are implemented.
